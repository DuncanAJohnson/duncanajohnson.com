I’m working at [teacher.dev](https://teacher.dev), solving one teacher’s problem per day. I build practical tools with educators, starting with the problems they encounter in their classrooms.

## EdTech-a-thon

I helped organize [EdTech-a-thon 2026](https://www.edtechathon.com/), a three-day collaboration between educators and developers held July 20–22, virtually and in Atlanta, Georgia. Teachers brought real classroom problems, and we built and tested solutions with their feedback throughout the event.

The tools we built became the first solutions in the [teacher.dev gallery](https://teacher.dev). My collaborations included Capitol Hill Crawler, a civics adventure; Dewey Dash, a library game; Evidence Room, a crime scene simulation; and Group Readers, a tool for matching students into book clubs.

Now I’m continuing that work one problem at a time. [Follow the daily builds at teacher.dev](https://teacher.dev).
